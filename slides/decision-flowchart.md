# Key message
Start with the research problem, not the platform

## Talking points
- This is the conversation we have with nearly every first-time client
- The first question is whether the cloud is the right tool at all
- Each stage after that has its own slide next; this is the map
- You can run this conversation at Rice without us in the room

## Slot: flow
- Is the cloud the right tool? No: campus HPC, or ACCESS and NAIRR on-premises resources
- Is CloudBank the right route? No: a departmental or institutional cloud account
- Allocation: ACCESS (Explore, Discover, Accelerate) or the NAIRR Pilot
- Onboard: CloudBank opens accounts on AWS, Google Cloud, Azure or IBM Cloud
- Work: build, run and watch spending
- Exit: archive, publish and close accounts
