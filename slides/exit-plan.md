# Key message
Plan the exit before the first instance

## Talking points
- The exit is part of the flowchart, not an afterthought
- Tags make cleanup possible: you cannot delete what you cannot find
- Decide early what is kept, where, and who pays for it after the award
- Publishing outputs is the cheapest way to keep them useful (Part 6)

## Slot: nodes
- Tag: Label every resource from day one
- Monitor: Review spending alerts every week
- Archive: Move keepers to cold storage
- Close: Publish outputs, then shut down

## Slot: caption
Awards end on a date; nothing shuts itself down when they do.
