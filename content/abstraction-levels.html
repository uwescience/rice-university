Every cloud offers the same three levels under different product names. Read the dark blocks: that is what your researcher owns. Moving right, the provider takes over layer by layer, trading staff time for a higher unit price.

Researchers from HPC default to the left column because it looks like the cluster; often a column further right is the better fit. CloudBank Classroom's JupyterHub is a platform you already met in Part 1.
